Subject: Request for guidance on licensing and lightweight offline distribution of the Madinah Mushaf page fonts (QCF) in a free app

Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullah,

I develop "Halaqati", a free educational Android app for Quran memorisation circles, used by many people in the Gaza Strip where internet connectivity is poor and often unavailable. The app displays the Madinah Mushaf (604 pages, 15 lines) using the Complex's QCF page fonts, obtained from the Quran Foundation CDN, so readers can study offline. We want to respect the Complex's rights fully and keep the Quranic text, diacritics, stop signs and page/line layout exactly as published.

The QCF page fonts we use do not carry an embedded licence text (the Uthmanic Hafs font does, and permits use, copying and distribution free of charge without modification). We would be grateful for your written guidance on:

1. Licence: under what terms may the QCF V1/V2/V4 page fonts be bundled in, or downloaded by, a free non-commercial mobile application for offline use?
2. Lossless packaging: may we compress the unmodified font files for transfer and restore byte-identical files on the user's device (no change to any glyph or table)?
3. Format conversion and subsetting: are format changes (e.g. TTF to WOFF2/CFF2) or keeping only the glyphs a given page uses permitted? If not, would the Complex consider publishing an official lighter distribution for mobile apps (the full set is about 208 MB, which is a barrier on weak connections)?
4. Rendering: may we render page images or vector outlines from the fonts for use inside the app (for example, to support smooth pinch zoom)?
5. Single-font option: may the Uthmanic Hafs font be used, unmodified, with our own line layout for a lightweight mode? Is there an official, authoritative list of which words appear on which line of each page of the Madinah Mushaf that we may use?
6. Attribution: what wording and placement do you require?
7. Contact: whom should we contact in the fonts/technology team for future questions, and is there any official application or developer programme for such requests?

We will not modify any font without your written approval. Jazakum Allahu khayran for your time and for your service to the Book of Allah.
